1. Information We Collect

PREFIOP, Inc. ("PREFIOP", "we", "us", or "our") collects information necessary to provision, maintain, and secure our modern field operations command platform. We collect information in the following categories:

  • Account & Organization Data: Name, work email address, company name, identity provider credentials (SAML SSO / OIDC tokens), billing contact details, and role-based permissions.
  • Field Operations Data: Job descriptions, service tickets, priority levels, technician assignments, dispatch schedules, asset records, maintenance checklists, and customer service locations.

2. Operational Data Protection & Governance

PREFIOP provides enterprise-grade data handling for distributed field workforces and customer locations. By architectural design:

  • All customer service history, asset maintenance records, and job locations remain strictly isolated within your organization workspace.
  • Real-time team location data and status updates are processed solely for active dispatch optimization and workflow coordination.
  • Workspace administrators retain complete control over team access tiers, export policies, and retention rules.

3. How We Use Field Operations Data

We process collected customer and operations information strictly for verified operational purposes:

  • To dispatch teams, coordinate active jobs, balance workloads, and track completion progress in real time.
  • To surface operational bottlenecks, measure SLA performance, and generate actionable management analytics.
  • To maintain audit histories of equipment inspections, safety checks, and customer sign-offs.
  • We do not, under any circumstances, sell, rent, license, or monetize customer field data or operational intelligence.

4. Cryptographic Multi-Tenant Isolation

All operational data, API transactions, and reporting records are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 with modern cipher suites and encrypted at rest using AES-256 with tenant-specific keys. Enterprise customers may integrate their own Key Management Services (AWS KMS or HashiCorp Vault) for sovereign encryption key lifecycle management.

5. Infrastructure & Authorized Sub-processors

PREFIOP utilizes premier cloud infrastructure certified under SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA compliance frameworks. Primary infrastructure partners include Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and Stripe for PCI-DSS Level 1 payment processing.

6. Your Data Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable global privacy statutes, you possess rights to:

  • Access, download, or export a portable copy of your account profile, job history, and operational records.
  • Rectify inaccurate personal profile or enterprise contact records.
  • Request permanent deletion ("right to be forgotten") of personal identities and associated records.
  • Restrict or object to specific automated processing pipelines.
