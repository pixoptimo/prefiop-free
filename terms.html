2. Operational Use, Dispatch & Fair Limits

PREFIOP provides real-time operational coordination for jobs, field teams, schedules, assets, and service locations. The following operational provisions apply:

  • Fair Use Limits: Plan-specified job quotas, active technician seats, and storage allotments apply on a billing cycle. Warning notices trigger at 80% and 95% capacity utilization.
  • Data Retention: Active job schedules, completed inspection logs, and asset service history remain queryable according to chosen tier schedules (30 days for Starter, 1 year for Professional, and custom multi-year retention for Enterprise).
  • Platform Integrity: Customers agree not to reverse engineer or interfere with the core dispatch algorithms, scheduling engines, or platform APIs beyond documented usage.

3. Service Level Agreements & Availability

For Enterprise tier subscriptions, PREFIOP guarantees 99.99% monthly service availability for field dispatch endpoints, mobile sync, and core web workspaces. Service credits are awarded upon documented unscheduled downtime exceeding specified thresholds:

  • < 99.99% to 99.90%: 10% monthly service credit

  • < 99.00%: 50% monthly service credit
