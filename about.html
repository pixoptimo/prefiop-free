✦ ABOUT PREFIOP

Built around the reality
of field work.

PREFIOP brings the moving parts of field operations into one clear workspace—so teams can plan work, coordinate people, track resources, and keep every job moving.

THE OPERATIONAL CHALLENGE

Field work is complex. Managing it shouldn't be.

Jobs happen across different locations. Teams move throughout the day. Schedules change, priorities shift, and equipment needs attention. When that information lives across disconnected systems, keeping operations on track becomes harder than it needs to be.

PREFIOP is designed around that operational reality. It connects the people, work, places, and resources behind every field operation into one unified view.

1 Unified operational workspace replacing disconnected point tools
Real-time Visibility across active field teams, schedules, and job status
Zero Friction between dispatchers, field teams, and service locations
